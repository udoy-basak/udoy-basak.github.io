The Kadomtsev–Petviashvili equation can be written as
$$
(u_t + 6uu_x + u_{xxx})_x + 3u_{yy} = 0.
$$


The governing equation is

$$
u_t + 6uu_x + u_{xxx} = 0
$$

where $u(x,t)$ represents the wave amplitude.


$$
\begin{equation}
u_t + 6uu_x + u_{xxx} = 0
\label{eq:kdv}
\end{equation}
$$

From Equation $\eqref{eq:kdv}$, we can analyze the traveling-wave solution.
