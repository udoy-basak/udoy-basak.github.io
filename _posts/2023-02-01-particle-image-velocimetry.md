*Scientific Reports*, **13**, 12566 (2023).

## Abstract

This study compares particle image velocimetry with the underlying dynamics of individual agents in collectively moving self-propelled particle systems. The work examines how collective velocity fields relate to the microscopic dynamics of individual agents.
